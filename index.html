HISTORY · DIPLOMACY · VIETNAM

Tracing the long road from war to reconciliation.

I’m Zachary Tayler, a Ph.D. candidate in History at Ohio University. I study U.S. foreign relations, the Cold War, and the history of U.S.–Vietnamese relations.

01 / ABOUT

History across borders and archives.

My work examines how the United States and Vietnam rebuilt a diplomatic relationship after the Vietnam War. I bring diplomatic records into conversation with the experiences of refugees, Amerasians, former political prisoners, and the people who shaped postwar policy.

I have conducted research in the United States, Vietnam, and Hungary, and use oral histories alongside archival sources to illuminate Vietnamese perspectives on the two countries’ changing relationship.

I earned an M.A. in History from Southern New Hampshire University and a B.S. in History and Secondary Education from Shippensburg University. My Ph.D. in History from Ohio University is expected in 2027.

02 / RESEARCH

The Lost Peace

Normalization of Diplomatic Relations Between the United States and the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, 1975–1995

Why did it take twenty years for Washington and Hanoi to restore diplomatic relations? My dissertation follows the negotiations, political pressures, and regional changes that shaped the path to normalization in 1995.

The project considers POW/MIA accounting, refugee movements, human rights, the fate of Vietnamese political prisoners and Amerasians, Vietnam’s intervention in Cambodia, and the end of the Cold War. It asks how humanitarian concerns could both delay and enable diplomatic progress.

04 / TEACHING

Making history a conversation.

In the classroom, I invite students to compare perspectives, work closely with primary sources, and connect historical questions to the world around them. I have supported introductory U.S. and world history courses at Ohio University.

Before graduate school, I taught seventh-grade modern world history at Stevensville Middle School in Maryland from 2018 to 2022. I also worked as a history tutor with Tutor.com from 2023 to 2024.
